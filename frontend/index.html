Under the Hood: Context-Aware Risk

VulnGraph AI integrates graph properties, version distance, and threat intel dynamically using this formula:

$$Risk = CVSS \cdot W_{cvss} \cdot (0.15 + 0.85 \cdot EPSS)^{W_{epss}} \cdot (1 + \text{PatchLag} \cdot W_{patch}) \cdot \left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{\text{Depth}}}\right)^{W_{depth}}$$

Formula Variables & Downscaling:

  • CVSS Base: Initial vulnerability severity score (0 to 10).
  • EPSS Factor: Exploitation likelihood. High EPSS keeps full CVSS; low EPSS scales risk down, but never lower than 15% to guarantee a baseline alert.
  • Patch Lag: Version distance calculated as (major * 1.0) + (minor * 0.25) + (patch * 0.05). Outdated code raises risk score.
  • Depth Decay: Direct dependencies maintain full risk score. Transitive dependencies are cut by 1 / sqrt(depth) to represent lower reachability.
